Know which products Google will reject, before you upload.

Feed Doctor scans your WooCommerce catalogue against Merchant Center rules and hands you a list: this product, this reason, this fix. No guessing which of your 2,000 items broke the feed.

The problem isn't generating the feed

Plenty of plugins generate feeds. What nobody tells you is why 340 of your products got disapproved, and Merchant Center's own diagnostics report the symptom, not the row that caused it.

The disapproval causes, in the order they actually occur:

RankCauseWhy it happens
1Price mismatchFeed price, currency or availability disagrees with the landing page. Sale prices, tax display and currency rounding are the usual culprits.
2Missing or invalid GTINEmpty, wrong length, or failing the check digit. Products are dropped or heavily limited.
3Image problemsToo small, watermarked, promotional text overlaid, placeholder, or a URL returning 404.
4Missing attributesRequired fields absent for the product's category.

Every finding names the product, the field, the actual value, and what to change. That is the whole product.

Built for European stores

The existing tools are broad and shallow — one advertises 220 channels. Feed Doctor goes the other way and is deep on the things that break European catalogues:

  • VAT-inclusive vs exclusive pricing, the single most common source of price mismatch in the EU
  • EAN-13 and EAN-8 check digits, not just "is the field filled in"
  • Per-country currency and decimal formatting
  • Sale price validity windows, which silently break feeds when a promotion expires
